2. Your data and your customers' data

When you use Simplek you enter details about your customers: their names, phone numbers, email addresses, GSTINs and addresses, and the invoices, quotations and payments you make for them. For that data your business is the Data Fiduciary. Simplek stores and processes it only on your behalf, to run the app for you, as set out in our Terms of Service. We do not use your customers' data for anything else, and we never sell it.

4. Who receives it

  • Supabase, which hosts the Simplek database, files and login, on servers in Mumbai, India.
  • Cloudflare, which serves the Simplek website and web app.
  • gstinapi.in, the GSTIN lookup provider: when you look up a GSTIN, we send that GSTIN to fetch its registered name and address.
  • India Post (PIN code lookup) and Razorpay (IFSC lookup): when you use these lookups we send only the PIN code or IFSC, never your personal data.
  • Accountants you invite: they can view (not change) your business data until you remove them.
  • Your customers, when you share an invoice with them by WhatsApp, email or a link.
  • Government authorities, only when the law requires us to.

Authorised Simplek staff can view your account details to provide support. Every such view is logged.

5. Where it is kept, and for how long

Your data is stored in India (Mumbai). It is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored encrypted.

  • While your account is open we keep your data so the app works.
  • When you delete your account (Settings → Your data and privacy) we wait 30 days, in case you change your mind: logging in during that time cancels the deletion. On day 31 all your personal data and your business data are deleted.
  • Sign-ups never completed are deleted after 30 days.
  • Security logs are kept for 1 year, then removed or anonymised.
  • Backups: deleted data may remain in our encrypted backups for up to 12 months, then it is erased.

GST records: GST law requires businesses to keep their invoices for several years. Before you delete your account, use Download my data to keep your own copy.

6. Your rights

In the app, under Settings → Your data and privacy, you can:

  • Download my data: a ZIP with your customers, items, invoices, quotations, payments and business profile as Excel and JSON, and every invoice as a PDF.
  • Correct my data: change your profile and business details at any time.
  • Delete my account: this also withdraws your consent (see section 5).
  • Nominate a person who can use these rights for you if you die or cannot act.
  • Raise a concern about how your data is used.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your account. Withdrawing is as easy as giving consent, and does not affect what was done before.
