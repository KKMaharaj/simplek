Why it's called Simplek

Simple, on purpose.

Most billing software is really accounting software, with screens you never open. Simplek leaves all of that out. It is only for creating sales invoices, and it makes that very easy.

What Simplek does

  • GST sales invoices and quotations
  • Payments received and what's still due
  • GSTR-1 report, ready for filing
  • Export to TallyPrime, Excel, CSV and PDF

What it leaves out

  • Purchase invoices
  • Stock and inventory
  • Ledgers and full accounting
  • Anything you'd need training for

Everything a sales invoice needs. Nothing it doesn't.

Simplek handles the paperwork of selling, so you can get back to the selling.

GST invoices

CGST, SGST and IGST are split for you from the place of supply, with HSN/SAC codes and automatic invoice numbers.

Quotations

Send an estimate first. When the customer says yes, turn it into an invoice in one tap.

Payments

Record cash, UPI, bank, card or cheque payments, in full or in part. Outstanding balances update themselves.

Share anywhere

Clean PDFs with your logo, signature and UPI payment QR. Share by WhatsApp or email, or print.

Ready for your accountant

Your GSTR-1 report builds itself from your invoices. Export it, or the whole period, to TallyPrime, Excel, CSV or PDF and send it across.

A login for your accountant

Give your accountant their own view-only access to your invoices and GST reports, on the web or the app. No sharing passwords, and you can remove it any time.
