Why pay for a full accounting suite just to send invoices?

Big software, small use.

Full accounting packages are built for accountants. Most owners only ever open the invoice screen, but pay for, and click past, everything else.

Hours of data entry.

Purchases, stock and expenses need daily entry and trained staff. When they fall behind, the reports are wrong anyway.

Invoicing can't wait.

No invoice, no payment. Billing should be the fastest thing on your phone, not something you have to learn.

Simplek does the part every business needs every day: correct, good-looking GST invoices, payments and dues. Everything else goes to your accountant in the formats they already use: GSTR-1, TallyPrime, Excel.

Why the name Simplek?

Simple + Lekka

In Kannada, ಲೆಕ್ಕ (lekka) means accounts, the everyday calculations of a business. Simplek is simple lekka: your business's numbers, kept simple.
